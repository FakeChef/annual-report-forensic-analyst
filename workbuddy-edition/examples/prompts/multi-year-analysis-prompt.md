# Multi-Year Analysis Prompt

```text
Use Annual Report Forensic Analyst — WorkBuddy Edition in Multi Year Forensic Mode.

Company: {{Company}}
Reports: {{Attach two or more annual reports}}
Analysis period: {{Start year–End year}}
Industry focus: {{Optional}}

Reconstruct the business model and value-creation logic, then explain what changed across the period. Test material management claims against disclosed evidence. Cover business model evolution, management narrative evolution, revenue/profit/cash trends, cash conversion, capital allocation, changing success assumptions, supporting evidence, contradicting evidence, risks, monitoring indicators, and open investigation questions.

For every material conclusion, use exactly this structure:
Fact: directly disclosed evidence with source location.
Inference: reasoned interpretation tied to the evidence.
Analyst View: independent judgment, uncertainty, and limitations.

Flag missing years, restatements, currency changes, reporting-scope changes, and comparability limits. Do not provide investment advice, stock ratings, target prices, valuation opinions, or unsupported forecasts.
```
