# Single-Year Analysis Prompt

```text
Use Annual Report Forensic Analyst — WorkBuddy Edition in Single Year Forensic Mode.

Company: {{Company}}
Annual report: {{Attach one annual report}}
Reporting year: {{Year}}
Industry focus: {{Optional}}

Analyze the annual report as evidence. Reconstruct the business model and value-creation logic; identify material management claims; test each claim against disclosed evidence; and assess financial reality, cash conversion, capital allocation, future success assumptions, contradictions, key risks, limitations, and follow-up investigation questions.

For every material conclusion, separate:
Fact: directly disclosed evidence with source location.
Inference: reasoned interpretation tied to the fact.
Analyst View: independent judgment, uncertainty, and limitations.

Do not merely summarize management statements. Do not provide investment advice, stock ratings, target prices, valuation opinions, or unsupported forecasts.
```
