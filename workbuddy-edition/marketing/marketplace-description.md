# Marketplace Description

## Long description

Annual Report Forensic Analyst is an evidence-based annual report research framework for enterprise research and management decision support. Upload one or more annual reports to reconstruct the business model, test management claims against disclosed evidence, explain changes across years, and identify uncertainty and follow-up questions.

Use it for CFO annual operating reviews, competitor analysis, management briefing preparation, strategy work on business-model and competitive-landscape changes, and consulting client research. General users can upload an annual report and start guided enterprise research.

The skill supports Single Year and Multi Year research, then converts completed research into PDF research reports, executive-slide briefs, or enterprise infographic briefs. These are output-conversion layers and require human review before formal or external use.

Forensic Analysis here means evidence verification, not legal investigation. Fact / Inference / Analyst View separates disclosed evidence, reasoned interpretation, and independent judgment. This is not investment advice, valuation, ratings, target-price, or legal-investigation software.

## Suggested triggers

annual report research; three-year annual report analysis; CFO briefing; management review; business model change; competitor analysis; consulting research; PDF research report; executive slides; enterprise infographic.

## Inputs

Upload one or more annual reports and provide the company, period, optional industry focus, audience, and desired output.
