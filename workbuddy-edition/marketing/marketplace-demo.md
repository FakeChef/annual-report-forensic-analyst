# Marketplace Demo

## General user: start enterprise research

```text
I have uploaded an annual report. Start enterprise research for {{Company}}.
Explain the business model, test important management claims against disclosed evidence, and separate every conclusion into Fact, Inference, and Analyst View. Include source locations, contradictions, limitations, and open questions. This is for enterprise research, not investment advice.
```

## CFO: prepare an operating review

```text
Use the completed annual-report research to prepare a CFO briefing for {{Company}}.
Focus on annual operating review, cash conversion, capital allocation, competitor context, risks, and monitoring indicators. Keep one evidence-led message per section, preserve Fact / Inference / Analyst View, cite sources, and flag uncertainty. This is management decision support and requires human review.
```

## Strategy: explain what changed

```text
Analyze the attached reports across {{Period}} for strategy review.
Explain changes in the business model, value creation logic, management narrative, and competitive landscape. Distinguish disclosed facts from inference and Analyst View. Do not reduce the work to a numeric comparison and do not provide investment advice.
```

## Consultant: create a client research brief

```text
Turn the attached annual reports into a client research working paper for {{Audience}}.
Provide an evidence matrix, management claims, supporting and contradicting evidence, Fact / Inference / Analyst View, limitations, and follow-up questions. Then propose a concise executive briefing structure. Review all claims and sources before client delivery.
```

## Output conversion reminder

```text
Convert the completed research into a PDF report, executive-slide brief, or enterprise infographic brief. Preserve sources, uncertainty, limitations, and Fact / Inference / Analyst View. These are output conversions and must be human-reviewed before formal or external use.
```
