# Marketplace Screenshots Guide

Use three screenshots in the Marketplace submission. Screenshots should use synthetic or anonymized data only; do not show private reports, real customer information, or copyrighted annual-report pages.

## Screenshot 1: Marketplace Card

Show the Marketplace card with:

- Display name: Annual Report Forensic Analyst v2.2.3 WorkBuddy Marketplace Edition
- Short description emphasizing evidence-based enterprise research and management decision support
- Audience cues: CFO, Strategy, Consulting, General User
- Clear disclaimer: not legal investigation or investment advice
- Visible version and Apache-2.0 license where supported by the Marketplace UI

Suggested caption: “Evidence-based annual report research for management decisions.”

## Screenshot 2: Research Workflow

Show a synthetic WorkBuddy conversation or workflow containing:

1. Annual report upload
2. Single Year or Multi Year Research Mode selection
3. A research request naming the company and period
4. A result excerpt showing Fact, Inference, Analyst View, source location, and limitation

Suggested caption: “Research Engine: upload reports, select a mode, and test claims against evidence.”

## Screenshot 3: Output Layer

Show a synthetic completed research result followed by a request for one output conversion:

- PDF research report, or
- Executive slide brief, or
- Enterprise infographic brief

Make the sequence visible: completed research → Output Layer → human review. Do not imply that the output is investment advice, an audit opinion, or automatically approved management material.

Suggested caption: “Output Layer: convert reviewed research into a delivery format.”
