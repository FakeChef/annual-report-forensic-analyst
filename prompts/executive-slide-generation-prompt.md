# Executive Slide Generation Prompt

This prompt is an output-layer tool. Slides are not summaries; they convert completed research conclusions into executive storytelling.

Create a 12-slide, McKinsey / BCG-style consulting deck:

1. Executive Judgment
2. Business Model
3. Value Creation Logic
4. Management Narrative Evolution
5. Supporting Evidence
6. Contradicting Evidence
7. Financial Reality
8. Capital Allocation
9. Success Assumptions
10. Key Risks
11. Monitoring Dashboard
12. Final Judgment

One slide must communicate one message. Use evidence-led titles and visuals. Preserve Fact / Inference / Analyst View, avoid decorative slides and generic AI language, and do not add investment advice, valuation opinions, target prices, ratings, or unsupported forecasts.
