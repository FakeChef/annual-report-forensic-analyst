# Forensic Analysis Prompt

Use Annual Report Forensic Analyst to analyze the following company materials.

## Inputs

- Company: `{{Company}}`
- Annual reports: `{{Annual Reports}}`
- Analysis period: `{{Analysis Period}}`
- Industry focus: `{{Industry Focus}}`
- Mode: choose Single-Year Mode or Multi-Year Forensic Mode.

## Instructions

Reconstruct the business model and value creation logic. Identify management's important claims, then test each claim against disclosed evidence. In Multi-Year mode, explain what changed across the period rather than only comparing numbers.

For every material conclusion, separate:

- Fact: directly disclosed evidence with source location where available.
- Inference: reasoned interpretation tied to the facts.
- Analyst View: independent judgment, uncertainty, and limitations.

Address financial reality, cash conversion, capital allocation, changing success assumptions, contradictions, and investigation questions. State missing data, restatements, scope changes, and comparability limits. Do not provide investment recommendations, stock ratings, target prices, valuation opinions, or unsupported forecasts.
